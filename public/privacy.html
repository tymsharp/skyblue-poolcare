Privacy Policy

How SkyBlue Pool Care handles the information you send us.

Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

When you request a quote we collect your name, phone number, address, zip code, email if given, and the service you need. We also record the page you came from and ad campaign tags so we know which ads work.

How we use it

We use it to quote, schedule and service your pool, and to contact you about your service by phone, text or email.

Text messages

If you check the consent box, we may send texts about your quote and service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We do not sell or share your phone number or text consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our customer management and messaging software.

Contact

Questions or requests to delete your information: zach@skyblue-poolcare.com or (310) 421-8863.
