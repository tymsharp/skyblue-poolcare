What Studio City pools need

Hillside pools

Pools in the Studio City hills are often tucked into small, steep lots with tight side yards. Your tech knows the access before the first visit.

Tree cover

Toluca Lake and the older parts of Studio City have mature shade trees. Leaves are the main fight here, so baskets get emptied every visit.

Flat-lot pools in North Hollywood

North Hollywood and Valley Village pools get full sun most of the day, so chlorine and stabilizer are checked every week.

FAQ

Studio City pool service questions

Do you cover North Hollywood?

Yes. North Hollywood (91601, 91605, 91606), Valley Village (91607) and Universal City (91608) are on our Studio City routes.

Can you get to a pool on a steep hillside lot?

Yes. Tell us about access when you request a quote and we'll plan for it.

How much is weekly pool service?

Weekly service starts at $XXX a month. The exact price depends on pool size, spa, trees and equipment. Send the quote form and we'll give you a firm number.

What happens on a weekly visit?

We brush the walls, steps and tile line, skim, vacuum, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, check the equipment, and test and balance the water. Then we send a photo and a full pool report.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Service is month to month. If you're not happy, you can stop any time.

What if you miss a week?

We don't. SkyBlue has a no skipped cleanings guarantee. If weather or a holiday moves your day, we tell you and make it up.
