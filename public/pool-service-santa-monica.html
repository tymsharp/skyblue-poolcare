Pool care in Santa Monica & Venice

Santa Monica pools live in ocean air. The marine layer keeps mornings cool and evaporation low, but salt air is hard on heaters, pumps and metal fittings. North of Montana has the larger lots; Ocean Park, Sunset Park and Venice have compact yards and spas.

Local knowledge

What Santa Monica pools need

Corrosion checks

Salt air corrodes heater parts, pump motors and metal fittings faster here than in the Valley. We look for it every visit.

Cooler water, slower algae

Cooler coastal water is easier to keep clear, but it's not immune. Weekly testing catches problems early.

Compact yards in Venice

Many Venice and Ocean Park homes have small pools or spas squeezed into tight yards. We service those too.

FAQ

Santa Monica pool service questions

Do you cover Venice and Marina del Rey?

Yes. Venice (90291) and Marina del Rey (90292) are on our Santa Monica route.

Why does my heater keep breaking near the beach?

Salt air corrodes heater parts. We check for corrosion and tell you what's worn before it fails.

How much is weekly pool service?

Weekly service starts at $XXX a month. The exact price depends on pool size, spa, trees and equipment. Send the quote form and we'll give you a firm number.

What happens on a weekly visit?

We brush the walls, steps and tile line, skim, vacuum, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, check the equipment, and test and balance the water. Then we send a photo and a full pool report.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Service is month to month. If you're not happy, you can stop any time.

What if you miss a week?

We don't. SkyBlue has a no skipped cleanings guarantee. If weather or a holiday moves your day, we tell you and make it up.
