Do You Need Weekly Pool Service in an LA Winter?

Algae slows down when the water cools, but leaves, wind and rain don't. What changes in winter and what doesn't.

Los Angeles pools stay open all year. Water cools down in winter and algae grows more slowly, so some owners wonder if weekly service can wait until spring.

What still happens in winter

  • Santa Ana winds fill pools with leaves and dust, mostly from fall through winter.
  • Rain dilutes the water and washes in dirt, which throws off the balance.
  • Leaf drop from sycamores and other deciduous trees clogs baskets and stains plaster.
  • Equipment still runs and still breaks. Freeze-protection settings and heaters need checking.

What changes

Pumps can usually run fewer hours, and chemical use drops. The weekly visit becomes more about debris and equipment, less about chlorine.

The cost of skipping

A pool left alone for a month or two can turn green, stain, or hide an equipment problem until spring. Getting it back costs more than the weeks you skipped. Weekly service is month to month with no contract.
