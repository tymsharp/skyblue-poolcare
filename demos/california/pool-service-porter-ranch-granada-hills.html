Pool care in Porter Ranch & Granada Hills

Local knowledge

What Porter Ranch pools need

Porter Ranch HOAs and gates

Many Porter Ranch communities have HOA rules for service vehicles and gate access. Tell us the rules and gate code and we work within them.

Mid-century pools in Granada Hills

Granada Hills pools are often original to 1950s and '60s homes, with older plaster and equipment. Our 21-point inspection after your first service shows what's worn before it fails.

Wind and ash off the mountains

Santa Ana winds come straight down from the Santa Susanas, and brush fires in the hills can drop ash in the water. After a wind or fire event we brush, vacuum and check the filter.
