Local knowledge

What Sherman Oaks pools need

Hard water

Los Angeles tap water is hard, so refills add calcium every week of summer. Left alone it shows up as white scale on the tile. We track calcium and keep the water balanced so scale doesn't build.

Hillside homes in 91403

Streets off Beverly Glen, Valley Vista and Woodcliff are steep and narrow. Pools there often sit above or below the house. We plan access before the first visit.

Smaller backyard pools

Many Van Nuys and Valley Glen homes have compact pools. They heat up and lose chlorine faster than big ones, so weekly balancing matters even more.

FAQ

Sherman Oaks pool service questions

Do you cover both Sherman Oaks zip codes?

Yes. 91403 south of Ventura and 91423 north and east are both on our Sherman Oaks route.

What's the white crust on my tile?

Calcium scale from hard water. We keep calcium in range to slow it down, and can clean existing scale.

How much is weekly pool service?

Weekly service starts at $XXX a month. The exact price depends on pool size, spa, trees and equipment. Send the quote form and we'll give you a firm number.

What happens on a weekly visit?

We brush the walls, steps and tile line, skim, vacuum, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, check the equipment, and test and balance the water. Then we send a photo and a full pool report.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Service is month to month. If you're not happy, you can stop any time.

What if you miss a week?

We don't. SkyBlue has a no skipped cleanings guarantee. If weather or a holiday moves your day, we tell you and make it up.
