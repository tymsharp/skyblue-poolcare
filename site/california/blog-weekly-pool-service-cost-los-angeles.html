How Much Does Weekly Pool Service Cost in Los Angeles?

What drives the monthly price of weekly pool service in the San Fernando Valley and Westside, and what should be included.

Most Los Angeles pool companies charge a flat monthly price for weekly service. The number you're quoted depends on a handful of things you can check before you call.

What changes the price

  • Pool size. More water takes more chemical and more time to brush and vacuum.
  • A spa. An attached spa is a second body of water to clean and balance.
  • Trees and debris. Oaks in Encino or canyon brush in Laurel Canyon fill baskets fast and add time to every stop.
  • Access. A hillside pool down a flight of stairs takes longer than a flat backyard.
  • Chemicals included or extra. Some companies include chemicals, some bill them separately. Ask.

What weekly service should include

At minimum: brushing walls, steps and the tile line, skimming, vacuuming, emptying the skimmer and pump baskets, testing and balancing the water, and a look at the pump, filter and heater. Filter cleans are usually extra and done a few times a year.
