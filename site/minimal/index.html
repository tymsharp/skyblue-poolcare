Services

Weekly care, cleanups and repairs

One local team for the weekly cleaning and everything that breaks in between.

Weekly Pool Service

Your tech comes the same day each week, cleans the pool top to bottom and balances the water. You get a photo and a full pool report after every visit.

  • Brush walls, steps and tile line
  • Skim, vacuum, empty baskets
  • Test and balance chemicals
  • Photo report after every visit

Filter Cleaning

A dirty filter makes the pump work harder and the water go cloudy. We break it down, clean it and check pressure before and after.

  • Cartridge and DE filters
  • Pressure check before and after
  • Less strain on the pump

Green-to-Blue Cleanup

Vacant rental, missed weeks, or a pool you just bought. We bring it back to clear and balanced, then you decide on weekly service.

  • Algae treatment and brushing
  • Filter clean included
  • Move-in and listing prep

Equipment Repair

When a pump won't prime, a valve sticks or the water level keeps dropping, we find the cause and tell you what it costs before work starts.

  • Pumps, motors and valves
  • Filters, plumbing and leaks
  • Clear quote before any work

Salt Cell Service

A salt system still needs care. LA's hard water scales the cell, and a scaled cell stops making chlorine. We clean the cell, check salt level and output, and replace it when it reaches end of life.

  • Cell cleaning and scale removal
  • Salt level and output check
  • Cell replacement, any major brand

Pool Automation

Pentair, Jandy and Hayward automation, installed and programmed. We set schedules that fit your pool and your power rates, and fix systems that stopped talking to the app.

  • Pentair, Jandy and Hayward systems
  • Schedules for pump, heater and lights
  • App setup and troubleshooting

Heater Repair & Install

A heater that won't light or shuts off early usually comes down to a few parts. We diagnose gas heaters and heat pumps, fix what's worth fixing and tell you straight when it's time to replace.

  • Gas heaters and heat pumps
  • Won't light, short cycles, error codes
  • New heater sizing and install

Tile Cleaning

The white crust on your waterline tile is calcium from LA's hard water. We remove it with bead blasting or pumice, depending on the tile, so the tile line looks like it did when the pool was built.

  • Calcium scale off the waterline
  • Bead blasting or pumice, matched to the tile
  • Spa and raised wall tile too

Drains & Acid Washes

High calcium, stains or water that won't balance sometimes needs a fresh start. We drain, clean or acid wash, and refill with a proper start-up.

  • Full drain and refill
  • Acid wash for stained plaster
  • Start-up chemistry after refill

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How much is weekly pool service?

Weekly service starts at $XXX a month. The exact price depends on pool size, spa, trees and equipment. Send the quote form and we'll give you a firm number.

What happens on a weekly visit?

We brush the walls, steps and tile line, skim, vacuum, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, check the equipment, and test and balance the water. Then we send a photo and a full pool report.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Service is month to month. If you're not happy, you can stop any time.

What if you miss a week?

We don't. SkyBlue has a no skipped cleanings guarantee. If weather or a holiday moves your day, we tell you and make it up.

How do I get started?

Send the quote form or call. We quote over the phone, a service manager does a full first cleaning, and after your first scheduled service we do a free 21-point inspection of the pool and equipment.

Do you service salt water pools?

Yes. We check salt levels and keep the salt cell clean so it lasts.
